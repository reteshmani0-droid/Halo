GOOD QUESTIONS

A little clarity
before you start.

Still curious? The getting-started guide goes into the details.

Which build is right for my computer?

ARM64 for Apple silicon and Windows on ARM. x64 for Intel or AMD. The download page recommends a build based on the computer you’re using and shows which platforms are currently published.

Is Halo really free?

Halo is free and open source. You bring the model it talks to: a hosted provider may charge for usage, while a compatible local model runs on your machine. There’s no model subscription included in the download.

Do I need to create an account?

No. The desktop app works without one. An account adds a dashboard for your connected machines and their reported messages, plus the ability to cut a machine’s access from this site.

Can the website control my computer?

No. The dashboard reads what your app reports and can ask it to cut access on its next check-in. The website cannot open a connection into your computer. Approval prompts for sensitive desktop actions stay on the machine.

Can I choose what Halo remembers?

Yes. Manage saved facts in the desktop app’s settings: search, edit, pin, export, or forget them. You can also turn automatic learning or the use of memories off.
