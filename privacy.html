Privacy

The honest version: the assistant runs on your machine, and this website stores only what the app sends it when you turn that on.

If you never sign in

Nothing about you is here. The site is static files: no analytics, no advertising, no third-party trackers, no cookies set by us. The only requests a browser makes are for the pages, the stylesheet, the scripts in this repository, and the Supabase script from a CDN.

If you create an account

Held in the Supabase project named in web/assets/js/config.js, which is the project the site's owner runs:

WhatWhy
Email and a password hashTo sign you in. Passwords are hashed by Supabase's auth service; nobody can read them, including the project owner.
Your nameSo a dashboard and an admin panel show a person rather than an address. You can change or empty it.
Machines: name, platform, architecture, app version, modelSo you can tell one install from another, and see what it is set to.
Messages you and Halo exchangedSo the conversation can be read from a phone. This is the sensitive one, and it is the reason the app's switch is off until you turn it on.
Timestamps and whether access was cutSo "is that machine still reporting" and "who cut it off" have answers.

What is never uploaded

Your files, your screen, your keystrokes, your model API keys, and the contents of your clipboard. The app cannot send them even by accident: the only things it posts are the rows listed above.

Who can see it

You. And, on a project where somebody has been made an admin, that admin: the point of the admin panel is to see the accounts and their machines. That is a deliberate choice you are making by signing in to somebody else's Halo project rather than running your own. If you want no admin above you, run your own Supabase project and your own copy of this site — it is the same files.

Stopping it

  • Turn the account off in the app's Settings → Account. Reporting stops immediately; nothing further is sent.
  • Delete a machine from the dashboard. Its reported messages go with it.
  • Delete your account: remove the user in Supabase → Authentication → Users. Every row belonging to it is deleted with it, by the foreign keys in the schema.

Not covered here

Your model provider sees every prompt you send it, as with any assistant that uses one. Nothing on this site changes that, and it is the honest place to say so.
